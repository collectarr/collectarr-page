## Loan Manager

Open **Loan Manager** to inspect loans and returns. Use the relevant entry/selection action to record borrower and loan/due dates. On return, record the return rather than adding another entry.

Use the offered status/date controls to find outstanding and overdue loans. Check the actual copy if duplicates have separate personal records.

## Statistics

Open **Statistics** for an overview of the current kind. Check its collection/status scope before interpreting totals. Counts can represent entries or quantities according to the displayed statistic.

Monetary totals depend on currency and stored values. Missing prices do not establish zero value, and statistics are not an appraisal or a guaranteed current market quote.

## Calendar and progress

The calendar and kind-specific tracking controls record reading, watching, listening or play activity. These are personal records, with different tracking models per kind. See the kind chapters for the relevant distinction.
