## Identify an edition

Check title, authors, ISBN/barcode, publisher, language, publication information and applicable edition details. Different printings/bindings can require separate entries even with the same work title.

## Your copy and reading

Keep status, condition, location, purchase information and notes personal. Independent entries help when copies have different personal records. Use book-specific reading/tracking controls for progress rather than altering catalog publication fields.

The shared [editing](../../editing/), [collections](../../collections/) and [custom fields](../../custom-fields/) chapters describe common controls.
