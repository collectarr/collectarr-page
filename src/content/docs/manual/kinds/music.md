## Album and disc ownership

Music is the kind name; **Add Albums** is the action label. An album represents a concrete edition containing discs and tracks.

Root fields include artist credits, title, release/original-release dates, label, country, barcode, catalog number, packaging, genres, box set, covers and links.

## Tracks and Disc Details

Use Tracks to add discs and edit their tracks. Add Disc stays on the right of the strip. Drag disc tabs to reorder them; review the contents before removing a disc.

Each disc owns format, technical data, recording date, recording locations, Live/Studio and SPARS. A mixed edition can contain studio and live discs with different recording details.

Known formats use presets. Custom formats require choosing the relevant family when offered. Families are vinyl, optical disc, tape, digital and other. CD/SACD are formats within optical disc.

## Credits

The single **Credits** tab edits Name, Role, Instruments and Applies to. Applies to selects Album or a disc. Roles are vocabulary values, not separate Classical/People tabs.

![Music credits with album and disc scope](../../../../assets/screenshots/credits.png)

Artist credits remain separate because credited names, join phrases and sequence have artist-attribution semantics. A track's Composition describes a work, not a contributor role.

## Personal information

Listening/copy information stays personal. Disc-owned local data is tied to the corresponding disc. Do not put listening notes into catalog recording locations.

## Groups and sorting

Disc-format and contributor groups use distinct contained values. Two CD discs give one CD membership; a Vinyl disc adds Vinyl membership.

Earliest/Latest Disc Recording Date provide explicit scalar sorts. See [Columns and sorting](../../columns-sorting/).
