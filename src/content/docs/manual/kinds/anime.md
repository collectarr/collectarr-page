## Catalog and tracking

Check title, contributors and available release/series data. Anime has its own metadata and tracking definitions; a similar TV title is not automatically the same entry.

Record episodes/progress/watch sessions in anime-specific controls. Use custom episodes where offered instead of altering unrelated fields.

Ownership, condition, location and notes stay personal. See [Editing](../../editing/) and [Backup/sync](../../backup-sync/).
