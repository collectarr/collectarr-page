## Get help

Ask a question in [Collectarr Community Q&A](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-app/discussions/categories/q-a).
Describe what you were trying to do, what happened, your app version and platform.

The community covers Collectarr App, Core and Sync. Start here if you are unsure which component needs attention.

## Report a bug

Use the [App issue tracker](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-app/issues/new/choose) for reproducible problems.
For server-specific problems, use [Core issues](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-core/issues)
or [Sync issues](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-sync/issues).

Include simple steps, the expected result and the actual result. A small fictional sample is often more useful than a complete collection.

## Suggest an improvement

Share proposals in [Ideas](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-app/discussions/categories/ideas).
You can also [browse the community](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-app/discussions) before starting a new topic.

## Before posting

Support conversations and issues are public and require a GitHub account to post.
Do not attach credentials, private databases or backups. Remove personal details from screenshots and logs.

## Release notes

Choose **Help → What's New?** in Collectarr for the release notes matching your installed version.
If that release is unavailable, the link opens the latest published release.
You can also [browse all App releases](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-app/releases).
