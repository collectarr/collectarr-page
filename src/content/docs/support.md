## Interactive Contact & Diagnostics Hub

For step-by-step guidance tailored to your setup, visit our **[Contact & Support Hub](/collectarr-page/contact/)**. You can choose between:
- **Mobile** (iOS & Android) — diagnostic paths, camera barcode scanning permissions, and mobile logs.
- **Web** (Browser & Docker) — console inspection, IndexedDB local storage, and CORS settings.
- **Desktop** (Windows, macOS, Linux) — local SQLite file paths, `%APPDATA%` logs, keyboard shortcuts, and backups.

## Report a Bug

Use the [App issue tracker](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-app/issues/new/choose) for reproducible problems in the client application.
For server-specific problems, use [Core issues](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-core/issues) or [Sync issues](https://github.com/collectarr/collectarr-sync/issues).

When reporting a bug, please include:
1. Your app version and platform (Windows, macOS, Linux, Web, Android, iOS).
2. The media kind you were working in (Music, Games).
3. Clear steps to reproduce the issue, the expected result, and the actual result.

## Before Posting

Support conversations and issue trackers are public and require a free GitHub account to participate.
> [!CAUTION]
> Do not attach credentials, private database passwords, or personal identifying information. Remove sensitive details from screenshots and log excerpts before sharing.
