## Volumes and publication

Check series, volume number/title, ISBN/barcode, publisher, imprint, language, publication date and page count. Authors, artists, genres, themes and demographic metadata describe the catalog item.

Keep date precision: a year can be known without month/day.

## Reading

Reading progress belongs to personal tracking. Condition, location, ownership and purchases describe your copy. Use custom fields for extra personal classifications rather than overloading genre.

Follow [Adding](../../adding/) and [Editing](../../editing/) for the common flow.
