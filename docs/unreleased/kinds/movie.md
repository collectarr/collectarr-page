## Film and edition

Inspect title, contributors and release metadata. Your media edition can also have region, packaging, distributor, features, HDR formats and box-set details. Matching the film title does not establish region or included media.

## Watching

Use movie-specific tracking for watch activity. Personal ownership, purchase and location remain separate from catalog film data.

See [Images](../../images/) for covers and [Collections](../../collections/) for organization.
