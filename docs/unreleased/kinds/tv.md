## Series and owned media

Check series match and edition. Region, packaging, distributor, features, HDR and box-set fields distinguish your media where offered.

## Episodes

Use TV tracking units and watch sessions for progress. Where offered, custom episodes belong in that tracking flow. Do not place watched-episode lists into catalog genre/title fields.

Collections organize named sets; TV groupings organize by supported field values. See [Search](../../browsing/) and [Editing](../../editing/).
