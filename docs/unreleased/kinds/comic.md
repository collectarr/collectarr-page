## Series, issue and variant

Check series/title, issue, publisher and applicable edition/variant fields. A series match alone does not identify a particular issue or variant.

## Your copy

Condition, grade, location and purchase details describe your own copy. Copies with different grading may need independent entries. Record reading activity in comic-specific tracking rather than changing issue metadata.

Groupings/columns use comic fields. See [Browsing](../../browsing/) and [Columns](../../columns-sorting/).
